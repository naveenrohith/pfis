# PFIS 85% Intelligence and Product-Stage Reassessment

Status: canonical forward roadmap  
Verified: 2026-08-03  
Supersedes the 70% target and forward plan in
`13-product-intelligence-maturity-roadmap.md`.

## Executive verdict

PFIS has progressed much further in **feature implementation** than it has in
**verified financial intelligence**. The repository contains most of the major
personal-finance concepts, but several are still thin read models, deterministic
thresholds, or single-provider implementations. This is why completing many
roadmap items has not produced a proportional increase in perceived intelligence.

The current state is best described by three separate measures:

| Measure | Current | Meaning |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Implemented product breadth | 72/100 | Most intended domains and workflows now exist. |
| Evidence-backed intelligence | 42/100 | Correctness, coverage, prediction, personalization, and outcome proof remain incomplete. |
| Product-stage readiness | 58/100 | The application is coherent and secure, but not yet proven as a dependable daily financial product. |

The lower intelligence reassessment is not a loss of implemented work. It uses a
stricter definition and incorporates newly identified account-balance correctness
gaps. PFIS should stop treating feature completion as intelligence progress.
From this point, score increases require representative evidence and a user
decision that measurably becomes safer or easier.

## What 85% intelligence means

PFIS reaches 85% only when it can reliably answer these questions for a user:

1. **What money and debt do I have now?** It distinguishes observed balances,
   transaction-derived estimates, pending activity, and unknown coverage.
2. **What changed and can I trust it?** Every material movement has an account,
   source, lifecycle state, and reconciliation outcome.
3. **What will happen next?** Forecast ranges are calibrated on representative,
   leakage-safe history rather than only generated by rules.
4. **What should I do?** Advice accounts for obligations, competing goals,
   uncertainty, reversibility, and measured past outcomes.
5. **Does PFIS learn safely?** User corrections and outcomes improve ranking or
   thresholds only after sufficient evidence, with rollback and versioning.
6. **Can it explain the answer?** A user can inspect the observation, movements,
   assumptions, missing data, and rule/model version behind a result.

85% does not mean autonomous money movement, perfect prediction, or a general
financial-advice chatbot. Those remain outside the safety boundary.

## The balance opportunity

### Short answer

Yes. PFIS can update bank and credit-card positions from the transactions it
already stores, provided there is a trusted starting balance and complete-enough
settled activity after that observation. The result must be called an
**estimated current balance**, not a live balance.

A genuinely observed connected balance requires a provider response. India's
Account Aggregator deposit schema includes `currentBalance`, `balanceDateTime`,
transactions, transactional balances, and pending transactions. AA access is
explicit-consent based, and production FIU participation requires an eligible
regulated entity or suitable partner path. This is a separate integration and
compliance program, not a parser enhancement.

Primary references:

- [RBI NBFC Account Aggregator Directions](https://systemhealth.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx_id%3D10598%281%29.html)
- [ReBIT deposit financial-information schema](https://specifications.rebit.org.in/api_schema/account_aggregator/documentation/deposit_v2.0.0.html)
- [Sahamati PFM and reconciliation use cases](https://sahamati.org.in/use-cases-for-account-aggregator-framework/)
- [Sahamati FIU/FIP eligibility FAQ](https://sahamati.org.in/faq/)

### Required terminology

| Label | Meaning | May PFIS claim it now? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observed balance | Amount supplied by a user, statement, or connector with an effective timestamp | Yes |
| Estimated current balance | Observed anchor plus eligible settled movement after its cutoff | Yes, after correctness work |
| Pending impact | Separate increase/decrease from pending or authorized activity | Yes, when the source identifies it |
| Live balance | Fresh institution/provider balance with known retrieval time | No, until a balance connector exists |
| Available balance/credit | Provider-supplied spendable amount after holds or issuer rules | No calculation should silently claim this |

### Bank account calculation

For an asset account:

```text
estimated current balance
= observed balance at cutoff
+ settled credits and refunds after cutoff
- settled debits after cutoff
```

Transfers and card payments must be included in an individual account position
even though they are excluded from spending analytics. Failed, declined,
reversed, duplicate, ignored, accounting-adjustment, unlinked, or pre-cutoff
rows must not silently change the estimate.

### Credit-card calculation

For a credit-card liability:

```text
estimated current outstanding
= observed statement/connector liability at cutoff
+ settled purchases, fees, interest, and cash advances after cutoff
- settled payments, refunds, cashback, and reversals after cutoff
```

PFIS must show four card concepts separately:

- billed statement amount and remaining amount due;
- estimated unbilled activity;
- estimated current outstanding;
- issuer-observed available credit, when supplied.

`credit limit - estimated outstanding` is only a rough estimate of remaining
limit. Holds, EMI limit blocking, issuer adjustments, overpayments, and pending
authorizations can make it different from issuer-reported available credit.

### P0 correctness findings from the reassessment

1. `FinancialPositionService.account_position()` filtered accounting adjustments
   but did not consistently require a settled transaction status.
2. Its snapshot-to-snapshot movement applied asset signs to every account.
   A credit-card debit purchase therefore reduced the calculated change when it
   should increase liability.
3. `AccountService.net_worth()` included unverified snapshots despite the
   verified-position product contract.
4. `AccountService._account_response()` exposed an unverified snapshot as the
   account's latest balance without identifying it as provisional.
5. Balance anchors used a date, not a provider-effective timestamp and timezone.
   Same-day movement could therefore be omitted or counted twice.
6. Current card utilization was based on statement due, not a current outstanding
   position. It was valid only as statement utilization and needed that label.

These are release blockers for estimated balance, safe-to-spend, current net
worth, and any recommendation that depends on current liquidity.

#### Implementation checkpoint — 2026-08-02

All six findings are now addressed in the working tree. Account movement
requires settled lifecycle states and applies asset/liability signs; verified
versus provisional observations are separated; date-effective balance anchors
are supported; and account position, Cash Plan, and Net Worth consume one
settlement-aware estimate. Card UI now separates issuer-billed due, estimated
current outstanding, pending activity, and estimated utilization. It also shows
paid-since-statement and signed net-unbilled activity as the ledger proof
between the billed anchor and the estimate; unreviewed rows keep the conclusion
in review.

The remaining balance-truth work is explicit rather than hidden: provider-backed
effective timestamps/source identifiers, connector coverage/cadence,
cross-account transfer coverage, representative reconciliation cohorts, and a
real connected balance provider (such as an eligible Account Aggregator path)
are still open. The connector-neutral observation endpoint and source-state
model now carry the contract needed for that provider, but they do not create a
provider connection. The legacy one-observation-per-calendar-day constraint has also
been removed so a connector, statement, and manual check can coexist as distinct
append-only facts; connector ingestion requires a stable source record identity.
Until a real connected balance provider exists, PFIS calls transaction-derived
values **estimated**, never live.

### Canonical balance-position contract

Build one server-owned read model instead of reimplementing arithmetic in each
screen:

```text
BalancePosition
  account_id, product_type, balance_kind, currency
  observed_balance, observed_effective_at, observed_source
  settled_movement_since_observation
  estimated_balance, estimated_as_of
  pending_increase, pending_decrease
  unlinked_count, unreviewed_count, duplicate_candidate_count
  coverage_start, coverage_end, latest_sync_at, coverage_complete
  reconciliation_delta, last_reconciled_at
  status, confidence, reason_codes, ruleset_version
```

Allowed statuses:

- `observed`
- `estimated`
- `stale`
- `incomplete`
- `needs_review`
- `unsupported`

An estimate never overwrites an append-only observation. When the next observed
balance arrives, PFIS stores the delta between observation and prior estimate,
attributes explainable causes, and uses the residual as accuracy evidence.

**Current implementation status:** the shared movement policy, verified-only
Net Worth, bank/card estimated positions, balance proof lines, source-coverage
fail-closed checks, Cash Plan fail-closed gating, retry-safe source-record
identity, and a connector-neutral balance observation envelope are implemented.
Provider observations now persist expected cadence, effective/retrieval times,
coverage windows, completeness, and freshness state independently of the
append-only balance fact. Connector batches now persist incomplete or failed
refreshes as source-health errors even when no balance fact was returned. A
non-zero snapshot reconciliation residual now also blocks Safe to spend rather
than remaining only a passive review detail. Imported bank/card legs now have a
bounded, deterministic candidate/confirmation path for cross-account transfer
linking; ambiguous candidates remain review-only and are never auto-linked. A
provider-neutral `BalanceSyncService` now validates mapped accounts, resumes a
consistent opaque cursor, ingests connector batches, and records non-secret
sync audit events; it still requires an actual institution connector. The
ReBIT deposit v2.0.0 parser/adapter now supplies a strict, retry-stable mapping
boundary for provider current balance and transaction-window coverage, but it
is deliberately transport- and consent-neutral. Credit-card provider
observations still require an issuer-specific adapter for current outstanding,
statement due, limit, and available-credit fields.
Guidance now grounds current bank/card, net-worth, and safe-to-spend questions
in those read models. Provider-backed coverage/cadence, cross-account
institution integration, reconciliation evidence, query evaluation, and
representative cohort measurement remain exit work.

The grounded query surface now also recognizes card-payment affordability
questions and delegates them to the card due-runway read model. It compares the
issuer-stated total due with the conservative lower funding forecast, exposes
the due date, shortfall, position state, and confidence, and refuses to call a
payment safe when statement, funding, or anchor evidence is missing. This is a
useful product behavior, but it remains implementation credit until the query
evaluation set and representative outcome cohorts pass the release gate.

### Phase 3 implementation checkpoint — 2026-08-03

`BalanceForecastService` and `GET /api/accounts/{account_id}/balance-forecast`
now provide the first account-level daily path. The read model starts from the
canonical observed/estimated position, applies explicit dated income,
confirmed obligations, mapped card-payment intentions, and sourced liability
schedules, and only uses a reviewed settled-history baseline after a minimum
sample. Expected, low, and high points carry source IDs, assumptions, coverage,
and shortfall/limit-pressure states. The Plan surface exposes that path. The
Cards surface now also consumes `GET /api/cards/{account_id}/due-runway`, which
compares issuer-stated total due with the explicitly selected funding account's
conservative path and keeps minimum due, estimated outstanding, available
credit, and planned payment intent separate. Missing statement, funding, or
anchor evidence fails closed. These slices raise implemented breadth, not the
verified intelligence score. Immutable daily forecast snapshots and exact-date
verified-outcome evaluation now provide the accountability substrate for
calibration; missing observations remain pending. The readiness endpoint now
reports frozen daily snapshot count, exact-date outcome count, interval
coverage, and mean absolute error, and keeps the personal forecast gate in
evidence-collection status until three outcomes exist. Representative
calibration cohorts, interval coverage, and risk precision are still required
before Phase 3 receives score credit.

The provider execution boundary is now visible through
`GET /api/balance-provider/status`: mapping, last successful observation,
cadence/freshness, and coverage are returned per owned account. The response
keeps `refresh_supported=false` and `consent_required=true` until a real
transport, consent/revocation lifecycle, and provider registry are configured;
the Accounts surface therefore explains why current amounts are observed or
estimated rather than live. This improves product honesty and recovery UX,
not the evidence score itself.

The next execution slice now persists a non-secret provider connection state
(`pending`, `active`, `expired`, `revoked`, or `error`), exposes consent status
without returning artifacts, and gates `/api/jobs/balance-refresh` behind both
an active consent and a registered connector factory. This is the durable
integration boundary; it still does not count as provider-backed evidence until
an eligible institution/Account Aggregator transport is registered and passes
the representative freshness/reconciliation gate.

Provider account identity is now isolated in
`BalanceProviderAccountMapping`, with unique user/provider/account constraints,
discovery as an explicit optional registry capability, and account-mapping
routes that require active consent. The Accounts surface can open a mapping
dialog when a registered provider has incomplete coverage; it shows only
sanitized provider candidates and refuses arbitrary duplicate identities. This
removes a correctness trap in which a generic connector identity could be
mistaken for a bank or issuer account, but it remains integration scaffolding
until a real adapter returns representative candidates and observations.

The card extension now has an explicit issuer-fact contract and append-only
`CardPositionObservation` record. Current outstanding can update the liability
position, while billed due, pending amount, credit limit, and available credit
remain separately attributable to the provider. This closes the data-model gap
for card refreshes but does not claim live evidence until a production issuer
transport and consented cohort are available.

The next balance-truth increment now persists immutable
`AccountBalanceReconciliation` intervals between consecutive verified
observations. Each interval records eligible settled movement, excluded
activity, expected versus observed closing balance, signed residual drift, and
review reason codes. This makes provider refreshes useful for detecting missing
or duplicated activity without rewriting historical evidence. A protected
cohort exporter and strict release-gate check now enforce attested
`production_safe` membership, minimum user/interval/institution coverage, and
published residual floors; no score credit is claimed until a real rostered
cohort passes those checks.

## Roadmap to 85%

The phases are sequential trust gates, not a list of parallel features. Score
bands are possible outcomes only after the exit evidence passes.

### Phase 0 — Financial truth reset (42 -> 48 band)

Deliver:

- Centralize eligible account movement with settled-status, review, duplicate,
  transfer, reversal, and balance-kind rules.
- Make verified and provisional snapshots explicit in every API.
- Exclude provisional snapshots from verified net worth and cash planning.
- Add effective balance timestamp, source timezone, provider/source record ID,
  and idempotent snapshot identity.
- Define bank, card, cash, investment, loan, and pay-later support levels. Do not
  estimate principal for loans without typed principal/payment evidence.
- Repair statement-utilization and current-position labels.

Exit evidence:

- Invariant tests for asset/liability signs and every transaction lifecycle.
- Snapshot-to-snapshot reconciliation examples with zero unexplained movement.
- Migration/backfill report showing ambiguous anchors remain incomplete rather
  than silently inferred.

### Phase 1 — Current position and continuous reconciliation (48 -> 60 band)

Deliver:

- Implement `BalancePosition` for banks and credit cards.
- Add billed, unbilled, paid-since-statement, current-outstanding, pending, and
  issuer-available-credit card fields.
- Propose and explicitly confirm paired transfers and card payments across both
  accounts; never auto-link ambiguous imported legs.
- Add a balance proof line in the UI:
  `observed anchor -> settled movement -> estimated now -> reconciliation`.
- Detect missing activity from the next observed-balance delta and route only
  actionable gaps to review.
- Base safe-to-spend on a current eligible position, with stale/incomplete
  states failing closed.

Exit evidence:

- At least 100 reconciled balance intervals across at least 10 representative
  users, 6 institutions, bank and card products.
- Median absolute reconciliation error below 1% of balance movement and 95th
  percentile error below a published safety threshold.
- The protected balance-reconciliation export passes the strict gate with a
  verified cohort manifest; missing/invalid manifests remain deferred or fail
  closed and cannot be counted as representative evidence.
- No unresolved P0 sign, cutoff, duplicate, or unsettled-status defect.

### Phase 2 — Coverage and source intelligence (60 -> 68 band)

Deliver:

- Turn the existing parser fixtures into independently attested production-safe
  cohorts; keep fixtures separate from release proof.
- Add statement adapters according to measured user demand, not provider count.
- Track expected-vs-observed source cadence and detect sync/history gaps.
- Add connector-neutral account and balance ingestion contracts.
- Run an AA/regulated-partner feasibility track: eligibility, consent purpose,
  retention, revocation, certification, FIP coverage, cost, and fallback UX.
- Preserve email/statement/manual routes so connected data is not mandatory.

Exit evidence:

- Parser release gate passes representative case/format/institution thresholds.
- Source coverage is known for the accounts used by balances and forecasts.
- A completed AA partner decision record exists; no “live” label ships before a
  provider-backed freshness contract passes.

### Phase 3 — Predictive cash and risk intelligence (68 -> 76 band)

Deliver:

- Forecast account-level daily balance paths, not only month-end spend.
- Use the implemented canonical balance-path read model as the single source
  for bank/card runway displays; keep its `needs_anchor` and `needs_review`
  states fail-closed.
- Compare card total due with a selected funding-account path, showing
  conservative cash-before-due and post-payment gap without claiming live
  issuer or bank state.
- Freeze daily forecast paths at explicit cutoffs and evaluate them only against
  later exact-date verified observations; leave unsupported points pending.
- Combine current eligible positions with dated salary, bills, card dues, EMIs,
  reserves, refunds, and confidence-weighted recurring events.
- Calibrate ranges by pay-cycle and account behavior; detect overdraft, card-due,
  cash-shortfall, and utilization risk before the event.
- Backtest every horizon and cohort using immutable cutoffs.
- Keep scenario simulation deterministic and separate from predictions.

Exit evidence:

- Minimum five representative eligible users with at least three completed
  periods each is only the first release floor; expand toward stable cohorts.
- Published error, interval coverage, calibration, and exclusion metrics meet
  the release gate by horizon and material user segment.
- Risk alerts meet precision/recall and alert-volume thresholds.

### Phase 4 — Decision and safe personalization (76 -> 82 band)

Deliver:

- Replace isolated recommendation templates with a constraint-aware decision
  planner over liquidity, due dates, reserves, debt cost, and user goals.
- Rank actions by urgency, expected impact range, reversibility, effort,
  confidence, and conflicts.
- Learn user-specific cadence and thresholds only after sufficient history.
- Use accepted/not-relevant/outcome evidence to adapt ranking with bounded
  effects, policy versions, rollback, and a visible explanation.
- Add counterfactual evaluation: compare action vs no-action baseline where the
  data supports it; never equate engagement with financial benefit.

Exit evidence:

- Representative recommendation cohorts meet minimum user/sample coverage.
- Helped/no-change/worse outcomes and automatic financial metrics are reported
  separately.
- No cohort shows a material safety or quality regression after adaptation.

### Phase 5 — Grounded financial interaction and product proof (82 -> 85+ band)

Deliver:

- Replace the keyword query router with a typed intent/query planner over
  approved PFIS read models.
- Support multi-step questions such as “Can I pay this card today and still
  cover rent before salary?” using the same position, temporal, forecast, and
  scenario services as the UI.
- Require citations to PFIS evidence IDs, as-of times, coverage limitations,
  calculation steps, and deterministic safety checks.
- Use a language model only for interpretation and explanation after tool/data
  authorization; calculations and decisions remain server-owned.
- Complete hosted monitoring, alert routing, backup/restore drill, performance
  budgets, incident ownership, accessibility, and representative end-to-end
  task validation.

Exit evidence:

- A versioned evaluation set covers supported questions, refusals, ambiguity,
  stale/incomplete data, and adversarial financial prompts.
- Grounded-answer factual accuracy and evidence citation meet published gates;
  unsupported questions fail closed.
- Representative users complete the core weekly tasks successfully, understand
  observed vs estimated values, and recover from incomplete data.
- Weighted intelligence score is at least 85 and every critical dimension is
  above its minimum gate; a high average cannot hide a financial-truth failure.

## High-value ideas unlocked by the same truth layer

These are not separate feature projects. They are consumers of current position,
temporal evidence, and reconciliation, in priority order:

1. **Balance drift detector:** infer missing or duplicated activity when the next
   observation differs from the prior estimate.
2. **Card due runway:** implemented as a reviewable read model and Cards-panel
   slice; calibration, payment-success linkage, and provider-backed evidence
   remain open before it earns intelligence-score credit. It shows whether
   current eligible bank cash and expected income can cover total due by the due
   date, without confusing minimum due with affordability.
3. **Billed vs unbilled card control:** show what is already payable, what will
   likely appear next statement, refunds in flight, and utilization impact.
4. **Refund/reversal tracker:** follow expected refunds through pending, posted,
   missing, or disputed states and update card/bank positions correctly.
5. **Pay-cycle balance path:** project the lowest balance before next salary,
   not only month-end totals.
6. **Commitment coverage:** identify which observed account is expected to fund
   each bill/EMI/card due and flag an unfunded obligation.
7. **Transfer integrity:** match both legs, distinguish internal movement from
   income/spend, and surface a missing counterparty leg.
8. **Interest and utilization risk:** estimate avoidable card interest or limit
   pressure only from sourced terms and outstanding evidence.
9. **Source health autopilot:** tell the user exactly which stale sync, missing
   statement, unresolved account, or truncated history weakens an answer.
10. **Decision simulator:** compare pay-card-now, preserve-cash, reduce category,
    or move a due date with deterministic before/after cash paths.

## MVP-to-product restructuring

### Keep

- The modular monolith, deterministic financial calculations, append-only
  observations, user ownership, source provenance, and fail-closed behavior.
- Today / Activity / Plan / Insights / Data & settings as the main mental model.
- The calm financial-product identity and observed/calculated/forecast language.

### Consolidate

- Break large orchestration hotspots such as `financial_position_service.py`,
  `intelligence_service.py`, and `temporal_event_service.py` into domain policies,
  read-model builders, and repositories without introducing distributed services.
- Split very large Plan components into task-level modules while retaining one
  coherent user journey; do not create a new card for every backend capability.
- Make one current-position contract feed net worth, cash plan, card workspace,
  forecasts, recommendations, and grounded queries.

### Stop

- Adding a screen whenever a model or endpoint exists.
- Raising intelligence scores for implemented plumbing without representative
  evidence.
- Treating synthetic fixture accuracy as production accuracy.
- Calling statement amounts current, transaction-derived amounts live, or
  unverified observations available cash.
- Expanding generative AI before balance truth, coverage, and evaluations pass.

## Product-stage release gates

PFIS is a product, rather than a broad MVP, only when all are true:

- **Truth:** bank/card current positions reconcile and fail closed on gaps.
- **Utility:** users can complete connect/import, review, understand current
  position, plan obligations, and act on one recommendation end to end.
- **Evidence:** parser, balance, forecast, anomaly, recommendation, and query
  cohorts pass versioned representative gates.
- **Safety:** every consequential number exposes source, as-of, state,
  uncertainty, and limitations; dangerous stale/incomplete states cannot drive
  advice silently.
- **Operations:** production database, migrations, restore drill, monitoring,
  alert routing, performance limits, incident handling, and deletion/export are
  exercised in the target environment.
- **Maintainability:** hotspot boundaries have owners, domain contracts, and
  change-safe tests; frontend task flows remain comprehensible and accessible.

## Immediate execution order

1. Fix the six P0 balance-truth findings.
2. Introduce the canonical balance-position schema and policy service.
3. Ship bank estimate and reconciliation, then card outstanding semantics.
4. Feed only eligible current positions into net worth and safe-to-spend.
5. Match imported transfer/card-payment legs through explicit review and collect
   reconciliation evidence across representative cohorts.
6. Calibrate the account-level forecast across immutable cutoffs and cohorts,
   then deepen decision models from its measured risk outputs.
7. Add bounded personalization and grounded interaction only after their inputs
   and evaluations are trustworthy.

This sequence converts the existing breadth into a compounding intelligence
system: each new observation improves position, reconciliation measures its
quality, temporal knowledge explains what comes next, forecasts quantify risk,
and outcomes improve later decisions.
